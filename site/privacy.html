term.hut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HutsonLabs ("we", "us") handles information when you use term.hut for macOS, Linux, Windows (WSL) and iPhone (the "App") and this website. The short version: term.hut talks only to the machines you connect it to, and we do not collect anything you do in it.

Information we do not collect

term.hut does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive, store or process what you do in the App. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your commands, terminal output, files, code, edits or agent sessions.
  • Your server list, host names, user names, passwords or SSH keys.
  • Your name, email address or contact information (unless you email us; see below).
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers. The App contains no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Settings, the list of servers you add, their trusted host keys and the App's SSH key are stored on your device. On iPhone the key is generated on the device, stays in the App's sandbox and never leaves it; only its public half is placed on servers you choose.
  • On your servers. The App connects over SSH directly to each server you add. Your shells, files and sessions stay on that server. To show them, the App installs a small helper program (hut-server) in your account on that server and runs your sessions in tmux there.
  • Passwords. When a server asks for a password so the App can install its key, the password is used for that one connection and is not stored.

Network connections the App makes

  • Your servers, over SSH, when you connect to them.
  • GitHub, to download the hut-server helper and, on desktop, to check for and download App updates from our public release page. GitHub sees the request (including your IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. No information about you or your use of the App is sent with it.
  • Services you open yourself, such as a link you follow from the editor.

Device permissions

On iPhone, the App asks for Local Network access so it can reach SSH servers on your local network. It is used only for connections you start. You can revoke it at any time in Settings; servers on your local network then stop being reachable from the App.

AI agents

term.hut shows the AI coding agents (such as Claude Code) that you run in your own sessions. Those agents run on your machines, under your accounts with their providers, and talk to those providers under their terms. We are not a party to, and do not receive data from, those connections. Please review the privacy terms of any AI provider you use.

Website

This website is a static site served by Cloudflare. We do not run analytics or set cookies. Cloudflare may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Support email

If you contact us at support@hutsonlabs.com or send feedback through TestFlight, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message (TestFlight feedback can include a screenshot and device details you choose to send). We use it only to respond to you and improve the App, and we do not add you to mailing lists or share it with anyone.

Children's privacy

term.hut is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal data through the App, no such data is retained by us.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your devices and your servers, you are in full control of it. Deleting the App removes its settings, server list and key from your device. To remove the App's helper from a server, delete ~/.hut_server (or ~/.local/share/term-hut/server) and the App's key from ~/.ssh/authorized_keys there. We hold nothing to delete on our end.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect personal data through the App, these rights are effectively satisfied by the controls above. For anything relating to an email you sent us, contact us and we will promptly honor your request.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy (for example, if a future feature introduces any data collection), we will update this page and the "last updated" date, and describe the change in the App's release notes before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@hutsonlabs.com.
